# QR Code Reader privacy policy

QR Code Reader processes an image only when you choose **Open QR link** from its right-click menu.

The selected image URL, downloaded image pixels, and decoded QR text are processed temporarily on your computer to open an HTTP or HTTPS destination. The extension does not store images, decoded links, or a reading history.

The extension does not send this data to the developer, a remote QR-decoding service, or an analytics service. It contains no analytics or tracking code.

Downloading the selected image contacts its original image host. The extension omits cookies and authentication credentials when requesting the image. If you open a decoded link, Chrome contacts the destination website normally. Those websites may process requests under their own privacy policies.

HTTP/HTTPS host access permits loading selected images from arbitrary image servers. The extension does not automatically scan pages or collect browsing history. Notifications are used only to report errors.
